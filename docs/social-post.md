# Day Three X post

On day three of a hospital stay, someone should check whether the antibiotic is still right.

I built Day Three to keep the evidence, clock, and responsible human connected until that review is
due. It reads synthetic lab reports, builds a local antibiogram, wakes at 48 hours, and prepares a
quote-grounded draft for a pharmacist. It cannot prescribe, dose, change an order, or contact a
clinician.

The part I care about most is what happens when the evidence is weak. Low-count cells show no
percentage. Hidden instructions are quarantined. Unsupported and empty quotes are rejected. The
limits are public too.
